About CPF Singapore

The Central Provident Fund (CPF) is Singapore’s national retirement savings scheme. At age 55, a Retirement Account (RA) is created from your Special and Ordinary Account savings to meet retirement income needs. You can set aside savings to meet the Basic Retirement Sum (BRS), Full Retirement Sum (FRS), or Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS) for higher monthly payouts in retirement. The ERS increases every year and is the cap you can top up your RA to once you are aged 55 or above.
