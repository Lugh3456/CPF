About CPF Singapore

The Central Provident Fund (CPF) is Singapore’s national retirement savings scheme. As you work, you and your employer contribute to CPF accounts — Ordinary, Special, and MediSave — which help with housing, healthcare and retirement.

At age 55, a Retirement Account (RA) is created from your Special and Ordinary Account savings to meet retirement income needs. You can set aside savings to meet the Basic Retirement Sum (BRS), the Full Retirement Sum (FRS), or the Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS) for higher monthly payouts in retirement. The current ERS is $440,800 in 2026 and is projected to rise to $456,400 in 2027, as CPF retirement sums are updated yearly. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The ERS is the maximum amount you can voluntarily top up your RA to for higher payouts, and it increases every January.
